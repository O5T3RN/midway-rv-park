About the Park

Everything worth seeing is twenty minutes from here.

Midway RV Park sits where the coast range meets the coast — close enough to Coos Bay for groceries and a diesel fill-up, close enough to Charleston for a fishing boat, and close enough to the Oregon Dunes that you can hear the wind moving through them from your site.

We're not trying to be a resort. We're trying to be a good, honest place to park your rig — level pads, real hookups, quiet nights, and a gravel road that doesn't beat up your undercarriage. Families, full-timers, and weekenders are all welcome here.
